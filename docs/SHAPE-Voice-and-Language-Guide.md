# SHAPE Voice and Language Guide
> Brand Guidelines — 2025

---

## Before You Write

Begin with a clear picture of what you want to say and who you're saying it to. Ask yourself:

- **Who is the audience?** Are you writing for employees, managers, or executives? Imagine you're speaking directly to one person.
- **What's the brief?** What's the core message or task? Stay focused, avoid unnecessary detours.
- **How should it look?** Would a list, table, or subheading make it easier to scan? Most people skim first, so structure matters.
- **What's the purpose?** Are you aiming to sell, educate, or engage? Match your style to that goal.

---

## Keep It Simple

Clear writing is simple writing. Cut what isn't needed. Choose the word that says it best in the fewest steps.

| ❌ Incorrect | ✅ Correct |
|---|---|
| on a daily basis | daily |
| is not | isn't |
| provide support to your people | support your people |

Simple doesn't mean plain. It means easier to read, faster to understand, and closer to how people actually speak.

---

## Our Voice

We speak with clarity, confidence, and humanity. Every line should feel natural, but precise.

- **Use UK English**
- **Use contractions** for a natural flow — e.g. *It's simple to use*, not *It is simple to use*. Skip them in legal or policy text.
- **Avoid "AI-sounding" phrases.** Words to watch for: *fostering, cultivate, unleash, driving, embrace, embark, unlock, tailored, thriving,* and "one ___ at a time" statements.
- **No em dashes.** Use commas or full stops instead.
- **Keep the tone** conversational, human, and professional.

---

## Core Rules

- **SHAPE** is always written in all caps
- **Explorers** are always capitalised
- Always write **well-being** with a hyphen
  - In body text (sentence case): *well-being* — e.g. "Employee well-being is central to performance."
  - In headings (title case): *Well-Being* — e.g. "The Science of Well-Being"
- **Oxford comma** in lists of three or more
  - ❌ We work with managers, executives and employees
  - ✅ We work with managers, executives, and employees
- **No comma** in lists of two
  - ❌ We work with managers, and executives
  - ✅ We work with managers and executives
- **Acronym plurals** take an *s*, never an apostrophe
  - ❌ KPI's
  - ✅ KPIs

---

## Headings

- No full stops
- **APA/Chicago-style title case** — capitalise nouns, verbs, adjectives, and adverbs; articles, prepositions, and conjunctions (a, an, the, in, of, at, on, with, for, and, but, or, to) stay lowercase unless they're the first word
  - ❌ Best practices for managers / Best Practices For Managers
  - ✅ Best Practices for Managers
- Keep under 7 words
- If more than one sentence, punctuation must be used

---

## Body Copy

Keep writing short, direct, and human.

**Write short sentences**
- ❌ Our reports provide detailed insights into the broader organisational context and are designed to help employees across all functions make decisions.
- ✅ Our reports give you clear insights. They make decisions easier for everyone.

**Avoid turning verbs into nouns**
- ❌ An analysis of the data was made.
- ✅ We analysed the data.

**Help readers skim** — break things up so they find the message quickly
- ❌ This platform is designed to give you everything you need, from performance insights to development tools, all in one place so you can stay on top of your goals and make progress faster.
- ✅ See your progress. Spot new opportunities. Act with confidence.

---

## Lists

**Bulleted lists:**
- No full stop for single lines
- Add punctuation only if a bullet runs over two sentences
  - ❌ Your results are ready. / Compare progress across teams.
  - ✅ Your results are ready / Compare progress across teams
- If the line runs longer than one sentence, treat it like normal copy:
  - ✅ This report shows where your team is performing well. It also highlights areas that need more attention.

**Feature lists:**
- Titles in bold, no full stop
- Descriptors are optional — if used, end with a full stop

| ❌ Incorrect | ✅ Correct |
|---|---|
| Team Report. | **Team Report** |
| Clear insights on group performance. | Clear insights on group performance. |
| Heat Map. | **Heat Map** |
| Spot pressure zones quickly | Spot pressure zones quickly. |

**Links:** Always underline links — e.g. [Download the Media Kit](#)

## Mascot Voice

The mascot is our friendly guide. Think of it as someone by your side — encouraging, clear, and easy to understand.

**How the mascot speaks:**
- Friendly, not pushy
- Warm and approachable, never salesy
- Light, not silly
- Positive energy without going over the top
- Sincere, not stiff — honest and human, with a clear purpose
- Helpful, not overbearing — short, simple lines that make things easier

**Writing style:**
- Always use sentence case
- Keep it short — one line or phrase
- End with a full stop
- Sound like a real person, not a script

| ❌ Incorrect | ✅ Correct |
|---|---|
| Please remember to carefully review your survey results for a better understanding of your personal flourishing journey. | Your results are ready. Take a look. |
| Do not forget, collaboration is essential for organisational success. | Working together makes progress possible. |
| Start your path to success. | You are ready to take the next step. |
